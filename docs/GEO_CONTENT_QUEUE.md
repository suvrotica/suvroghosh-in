# GEO Content-Improvement Queue — suvroghosh.in

This queue selects a small, professionally relevant part of the archive for evidence, attribution,
media-description, and internal-link improvements. It is not a keyword-volume plan. No search-volume
data was available, and no volume has been invented. Fiction, satire, and memoir are excluded from
this factual-citation programme unless a specific factual claim later needs clarification.

Queue created: 2026-07-22. The editorial pilot described below is **not yet deployed**; its
production deployment date will become the measurement day zero only after the revised pages pass
local verification and are deliberately released.

## Scoring method

Each page receives 1–5 points on eight qualitative dimensions, for a maximum of 40:

- **P — Professional relevance:** connection to healthcare IT, clinical data, interoperability,
  analytics, applied AI, or demonstrable technical work.
- **I — Reader intent:** how clearly the page answers a question a practitioner, hiring manager,
  collaborator, or technically curious reader might deliberately ask.
- **E — Expertise:** strength of the author's original expertise or first-hand basis.
- **D — Distinctiveness:** ability to make a defensible contribution beyond a generic explainer.
- **C — Citation potential:** likelihood that a careful search or answer engine could usefully cite
  the page after evidence improvements.
- **G — Evidence gap:** remediation urgency; 5 means the current evidence/provenance gap is large.
- **L — Internal-link importance:** importance within the site's topic graph and professional paths.
- **V — Conversion relevance:** connection to consulting, professional opportunities,
  subscriptions, the résumé, projects, or contact.

The dimensions intentionally value first-hand knowledge and defensible contribution alongside
retrieval intent. They do not predict rankings or citations.

## Why the first pilot is ranks 1–4

The first four pages cover a useful professional range—legacy clinical data, interoperability,
causal analytics, and machine-learning representations—while sharing a tractable remediation
pattern. Each is a canonical, high-conversion page with strong first-hand or architecture-level
potential, but the pre-pilot version had no external evidence links, only a `Related Posts` heading,
and a lead `<Pi>` image without authored alt text or a caption.

Each also has an older redirected sibling whose author-written headings and reference notes can be
selectively consolidated without replacing the author's voice:

| Canonical pilot page            | Redirected sibling available for careful consolidation | Distinctive thesis to preserve                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| VA MUMPS → SQL                  | `how-va-healthcare-data-systems-work.md`               | Moving from MUMPS/FileMan to SQL is semantic translation, not merely database conversion; the warehouse needs an interpretation contract. |
| HIE from First Principles       | `hie-from-first-principles.md`                         | An HIE is governance of identity, terminology, provenance, and time—not simply a data pipe.                                               |
| Confounding Factors             | `confounding-factors.md`                               | Workflow, eligibility, and denominator choices manufacture confounding upstream, before a model is fitted.                                |
| Latent Space in Healthcare Data | `latent-space-healthcare-data.md`                      | Unresolved identity, time, terminology, and provenance become geometry; stable vectors can still be clinically false.                     |

Rank 5, _The Questions You Ask_, is the reserve if a fifth page is added. Its value is a
practice-based comparison of fragmented academic medicine with longitudinal VA care. Any revision
must say clearly that it is the author's analytical perspective—not an official, current, or
exhaustive description of either institution.

FHIR is deliberately in wave 2 despite its high relevance. It already has twelve headings,
authored alt text, and three source links, so its marginal evidence gain is lower than the first
cohort.

For every pilot page, the acceptance criteria are:

1. State the original thesis near the opening without replacing the article with generic prose.
2. Distinguish primary evidence, professional experience, analysis/inference, and illustrative or
   composite examples.
3. Add only directly relevant authoritative sources and a visible `Sources and further reading`
   section where justified.
4. Add authored alt text and captions for meaningful original media; decorative media may use an
   empty alt value.
5. Set `dateModified` only after a substantive editorial revision is complete.
6. Preserve redirects, internal links, canonical identity, and the original publication date.
7. Run local regression checks before release, then compare this cohort with untouched pages at
   30, 60, and 90 days.

## Expansion rule

Do not expand automatically from the pilot to the remaining fifteen pages. At day 30, review
indexing, impressions, cited URLs, qualified referrals, engagement, and professional conversions.
At day 60, compare direction and page-level retrieval with a matched group of untouched pages. At
day 90, decide whether the treatment earned enough trustworthy discovery or reader value to extend,
modify, or stop. Small numbers should be reported as small numbers; no single citation or visit is
a trend.
