# Authority and distribution

## Executive conclusion

External authority is **plausibly a constraint, but backlink shortage is not verified**. The public evidence shows a sound owned identity foundation, one observed owned repository link, small referrals from owned/social channels, and a few credible publication/professional records. It does not show a measured portfolio of independent editorial, institutional, scholarly, or community backlinks to the current domain. Google Search Console Links and Bing Webmaster backlinks—the primary datasets needed to test that proposition—were unavailable.

The retained table shows **sparse attributable distribution; overall distribution remains unverified**. Only 19 listed referrer visitors and 23 views were attributed across all displayed referrers in 28 days: Google 7/9, LinkedIn 3/4 across web and Android, DuckDuckGo 2/2, MSN New Tab 2/3, YouTube 2/2, and Bing, Substack, and Vercel 1/1 each (visitors/views). These counts are aggregate, traffic is confounded, source pages and target pages are unavailable, and none is evidence of a qualified human visit or an earned backlink. The unlisted remainder cannot be classified as direct, referral, or undistributed traffic from this table.

No outreach, post, submission, account change, link request, or automated distribution was performed.

## What is verified

- The site declares exact LinkedIn, Substack, YouTube, and X profile URLs through its Person entity. External ownership/content/metrics were not authenticated.
- The audited [GitHub repository](https://github.com/suvrotica/suvroghosh-in) provides owned technical provenance and an observed link to the site.

- A [CIOReview ClinZen record](https://www.cioreview.com/clinzen-llc) and [ACN Newswire ClinZen interview](https://www.acnnewswire.com/press-release/english/22080/clinical-trial-efficiency-should-be-a-key-focus) align on name, company, and role, but require owner confirmation before any identity linkage.

## What remains unverified

- Bing Webmaster backlinks, Site Explorer references, and any Bing/Chat contribution.
- Whether the owned social/newsletter profiles visibly link back to the canonical site today.
- Exact source pages and landing pages for aggregate Vercel referrals.
- Follow/nofollow/sponsored attributes for all candidate references.
- Qualified referral traffic, returning readership, subscriptions, consulting actions, contact actions, and substantive enquiries.
- Whether any public record belongs to the site owner where only a common name, company, and role align.

## Authority gaps that matter

1. **Authoritative link data is absent.** Public exact-domain and exact-title searches cannot measure a backlink graph.
2. **Professional proof is not yet connected safely.** Publication and ClinZen records may corroborate genuine work, but owner confirmation and precise contribution context must precede linkage.
3. **Name ambiguity weakens entity resolution.** Exact IDs, employer/project context, DOI, and stable owned profile URLs are more useful than repeating the name.
4. **Evidence-sensitive healthcare pages are not outreach-ready.** Primary/institutional support and clear fact/inference/experience boundaries come before promotion.
5. **Interactive work lacks reference-ready packaging.** Educators and developers can cite a stable method, model assumptions, source, licence, accessible fallback, and screenshot more readily than an unexplained live canvas.
6. **Conversion attribution is absent.** Authority work cannot yet be tied to enquiries, subscriptions, or qualified professional outcomes.

## Distribution operating model

Use a maximum of one healthcare and one interactive flagship in each six-week cycle. A page passes the readiness gate only when its evidence, author contribution, limitations, mobile/accessibility, static explanation, canonical, and conversion path have been reviewed. Essays use a separate subscriber/editorial path and should not be forced into non-brand search intent.

For each ready page:

1. Publish a useful native summary or demonstration on one confirmed owned channel.
2. Make a substantive, audience-specific contribution in at most five carefully chosen communities or curated-resource contexts; the contribution comes before the link.
3. Ask for corrections or usage feedback, not a backlink.
4. Record channel, placement type, date, canonical link, and any channel-supplied aggregate results. Keep people-level outreach records private.
5. Review after six weeks using only attributable evidence. Stop where community rules prohibit promotion or traffic remains dominated by owner/tests/automation.

The complete manual cadence is in `MANUAL_DISTRIBUTION_PLAN.md`. Ready-to-edit drafts for all 25 provisional flagships are in `FLAGSHIP_SHARE_PACKETS.md`.

## Measurement and decision rules

Track earned referring domains only after Google/Bing exports are available, then separate owned, social, directory, editorial, institutional, scholarly, community, low-quality, and suspicious sources. Track Vercel referred visits directionally, but do not call them qualified until a clean traffic baseline and privacy-respecting outcome events exist. Use Search Console/Bing impressions and clicks, indexed flagship count, repeat readership, consulting/resume actions, contact actions, and substantive enquiries when those datasets become available.

The proposed UTM convention in `FLAGSHIP_SHARE_PACKETS.md` is a naming standard, not current measurement: the authenticated Vercel Hobby interface did not expose UTM reporting or custom events. Prefer the bare canonical URL where a community disallows tracking parameters.

Do not buy or exchange links at scale, automate outreach, submit to mass directories, create fake profiles, manufacture engagement, claim mentions as endorsements, add unconfirmed identities to `sameAs`, expose client information, or promote evidence-sensitive pages before review.
