# Authority gaps

## Highest-impact gaps

1. **Backlink evidence is unavailable.** Google Search Console Links and Bing Webmaster Tools are the primary missing datasets. Do not diagnose “no backlinks” from public search.
2. **Professional corroboration is weakly connected to the canonical entity.** The resume has a DOI, while other matching ClinZen records are not currently contextualized. Owner confirmation must precede any linkage.
3. **Name ambiguity is material.** Public results include many unrelated people named Suvro Ghosh. Stable Person IDs and exact profile URLs help, but consistent employer/project/publication references across owned profiles are still needed.
4. **The consulting surface intentionally has analyses, not client case studies.** That is honest, but it limits outcome proof. Confidential or fabricated case studies are not acceptable; anonymized, permissioned evidence is an owner/editorial project.
5. **Many high-stakes healthcare pages have evidence gaps.** Topical authority requires primary/institutional sources mapped to consequential claims, not merely more internal links.
6. **Distinctive interactive work has early discovery but little external citation evidence.** Reproducible methodology, embeddable explanations, educator notes, and source repositories can make those pages legitimately referenceable.
7. **Conversion is not measured.** Authority activity cannot be connected to enquiries or other outcomes yet.

## Recommended proof assets

- One concise, owner-approved professional identity block reused across the site and exact owned profiles.
- Publication/project pages that connect claims to DOI, methods, constraints, and the author's precise contribution.
- Two to four permissioned case notes or architecture walkthroughs; label public analyses as analyses, not client outcomes.
- Stable methodology and citation sections on each promoted healthcare/science flagship.
- An accessible static explanation and shareable figure for each interactive flagship.
- A verified GitHub profile/repository connection if the owner confirms it.
- Search Console/Bing link exports joined by target URL and linking domain.

## What not to do

Do not buy links, exchange links at scale, automate outreach, add unverified `sameAs` identities, claim mentions as endorsements, expose client/confidential data, or rewrite literary work into link bait.
