# Executive diagnosis

Audit date: **2026-08-06 (Asia/Calcutta)**

Production baseline: commit `1952c2d523bf4b6bbd352d8e8c041b912ae9ae89`, verified as the current Vercel production deployment at the audit snapshot.

## Bottom line

The reported **20–24 visitors per day is plausible as a Vercel counter, but it is not presently trustworthy as a human-audience, organic-search, or business-outcome baseline**. Vercel recorded 164 period visitors over its retained seven-day view, or 23.4 per day, and 913 visitors with 3,394 views in the exact 28-day audit window. The same evidence contains a concentrated 16–17 July burst, unusual country/device combinations, extensive development activity, unknown owner/test traffic, almost no attributable referrer data, and no conversion events. The correct status is **OBSERVED BUT CONFOUNDED**, not “fake traffic” and not “23 genuine readers per day.” Evidence: `ANALYTICS_TRUTH.md` sections “Verdict”, “Contamination and anomaly analysis”, and “Search-versus-analytics boundary”; `TRAFFIC_BY_DAY.csv` rows `2026-07-16` and `2026-07-17`.

No blacklist, manual action, malware warning, hacked-site state, or search-engine penalty was verified. Google Safe Browsing, Spamhaus DBL, and SURBL returned explicit clean results for their own narrow scopes. Google Search Console Manual Actions and Security Issues, Bing security reports, SmartScreen, VirusTotal, URLhaus, and social-platform blocking remain unverified because authoritative access or a usable public verdict was unavailable. Public Google results exposed the home page, professional pages, hubs, and individual articles, which rules out complete Google disappearance at the observation time but does not prove full index coverage. Evidence: `BLACKLIST_AND_REPUTATION.md` sections “Executive verdict” and “Service evidence matrix”; `GOOGLE_SEARCH_CONSOLE.md` section “What public evidence does establish”; `PUBLIC_SEARCH_TESTS.csv`.

The most defensible current diagnosis is a combination of **analytics contamination, missing search-engine evidence, verified desktop loading weakness, unmeasured conversion, evidence/readiness work on selected factual pages, and limited measurable distribution**. The accepted live crawl rules out a site-wide crawl block or disconnected canonical graph under the audit contract, but the audit still cannot allocate low traffic among engine indexing, ranking, demand, authority, and conversion because Google/Bing account evidence is unavailable and the audience counter is confounded. This is an evidence boundary, not a reason to infer that every layer is defective.

## Principal constraints, in evidence order

1. **Decision-quality measurement is unavailable.** The raw counter cannot separate human demand from owner, development, synthetic, or unknown automated activity, and Google/Bing clicks are unavailable for reconciliation.
2. **Search-engine account evidence is blocked.** Without Search Console and Bing exports, the audit cannot distinguish technical exclusion, no impressions, weak position, weak CTR, or low demand at portfolio scale.
3. **Desktop loading is materially weak, including a professional landing path.** Field p75 LCP was 6.96 seconds; the résumé initially transfers roughly 981–1,004 KiB of JavaScript and eagerly instantiates three YouTube players.
4. **Selected factual and interactive pages need evidence/readiness work before promotion.** The corpus audit surfaces high-requirement evidence gaps and the performance/accessibility audit identifies concrete route defects; those are review queues, not blanket editorial verdicts.
5. **Independent authority and distribution are weakly evidenced and conversion is unmeasured.** Public evidence shows few attributable referrals and no measured backlink graph or qualified outcomes, so code-only work cannot be assumed to produce growth.

These are current constraints and evidence gaps, not five proven causal shares of low traffic. The accepted crawl narrows technical work to exact P2/P3 rows and does not displace the evidence-order constraints above; engine indexing remains **UNVERIFIED — REQUIRES OWNER ACTION**.

## Highest-leverage next decisions

1. Obtain read-only Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster evidence, including Manual Actions, Security Issues, performance, indexing, sitemaps, links, Core Web Vitals, and the 45-URL inspection sample. Do not change ownership or DNS without explicit owner authorization.
2. Freeze a privacy-reviewed measurement contract and collect 30 clean days with owner, deploy, audit, preview, monitoring, and known synthetic activity excluded or annotated. Implement only the smallest approved conversion-event set.
3. Decide the exact crawl-derived P2 proposals only: the four canonical-policy rows, two notebook dead ends, one hidden-only resource path, and two shared legacy topic hrefs. Preserve page purpose and require a bounded recrawl; do not mass-edit canonicals, redirects, noindex rules, archives, or hub links.
4. Fix and verify the highest-value loading path first: accessible click-to-play résumé videos, followed by responsive archive images and the measured visualization issues. Preserve canonicals, initial explanatory text, keyboard access, captions, and static fallbacks.
5. Clear evidence and accessibility holds for at most one healthcare and one interactive flagship, then run one small manual distribution cycle. Measure attributable use and genuine references; do not publish more by default, mass-edit the archive, buy links, or automate outreach.

Detailed actions, dependencies, owners, and close conditions are maintained in `OWNER_ACTIONS.md`, `THIRTY_SIXTY_NINETY_DAY_PLAN.md`, and `REMEDIATION_BACKLOG.csv` rather than duplicated here.
