# Flagship share packets

These are owner-editable drafts for all 25 provisional rows in `FLAGSHIP_PAGES.csv`. Nothing here was posted, sent, submitted, or used to contact a third party. A draft marked **HOLD** must not be distributed until its evidence, accuracy, contribution, performance, and accessibility gate is cleared by the owner/editor.

## Convention for every packet

- Use the bare canonical URL when a community prohibits tracking parameters or when UTM reporting is unavailable.
- If a future approved analytics setup can report UTMs, use `utm_source=<channel>`, `utm_medium=<social|newsletter|video|community>`, `utm_campaign=flagship_2026_q3`, and the packet's `utm_content` value. This is only a naming convention: the current Vercel Hobby view did not expose UTM reports or custom events.
- Keep the native post useful without requiring a click. Ask for corrections or teaching/use feedback, not a backlink.
- Do not claim traffic, ranking, medical validity, institutional endorsement, client outcomes, or affiliation unless separately verified.
- Every image or clip needs descriptive alt text or captions. Do not include confidential records, real patient data, private dashboards, or unlicensed third-party media.
