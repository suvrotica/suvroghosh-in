# Manual distribution plan

No post, message, email, submission, or account change was made. This is an owner-executed plan.

## Operating principle

Distribute a small evidence-ready flagship set. Promotion does not repair weak evidence, poor performance, or unclear audience fit; those readiness gates come first.

| Track                | Pages                                                               | Primary audiences                                                   | Owner-controlled channel                 | Earned channel hypothesis                                              | Readiness gate                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Healthcare authority | Provisional rows in `FLAGSHIP_PAGES.csv`, only after owner approval | Healthcare IT architects, data teams, clinical informatics, vendors | LinkedIn, YouTube demos, consulting page | Standards/health-informatics communities, conference/resource curators | Claims sourced; contribution/limitations explicit; owner-approved                 |
| Science/interactive  | Provisional interactive candidates, only after owner approval       | Developers, educators, students, visualization practitioners        | Site, GitHub, YouTube, LinkedIn          | Technical/visualization communities and educator newsletters           | Fast usable lab; methods; static fallback; accessible explanation; owner-approved |
| Essays               | Five provisional essay candidates, only after owner approval        | Existing readers and literary/cultural audiences                    | Substack, X, writing hub                 | Human-edited newsletters/publications                                  | Preserve voice; no keyword rewrite; excerpt rights clear; owner-approved          |

## Six-week manual cadence

- Week 1: finish evidence/performance work on one healthcare and one interactive flagship; define conversion measurement.
- Week 2: publish one first-party LinkedIn/Substack note per ready page, using canonical URLs and truthful caveats.
- Week 3: make a short demonstration or annotated image for the interactive page; link back to the full methodology.
- Week 4: approach at most five tightly relevant curators/educators/editors individually. Explain why their audience might use the resource; do not ask for a link as the primary value exchange.
- Week 5: publish one essay through the newsletter and one professional follow-up that answers a real question raised by readers.
- Week 6: review engine clicks, qualified referrals, engagement, and outcomes. Continue only formats that produce attributable value.

Repeat with a maximum of two flagship pages per cycle. Do not promote all 25 simultaneously.

## Measurement

Record page, date, channel, placement type, canonical link, impressions/clicks supplied by that channel, Vercel referred visits, and approved outcomes. Keep people-level outreach details private. UTM detail is unavailable in the current Vercel plan; do not pretend tagged links are measurable until the analytics stack supports them.

## Stop conditions

Stop or revise a distribution track if the page is evidence-sensitive and not reviewed, the interactive experience fails mobile/accessibility checks, a community disallows self-promotion, outreach requires personal-data harvesting, or observed traffic remains dominated by tests/automation.
