The publication “Task Overlap Among Primary Care Team Members” has a public DOI and a third-party publication record listing Suvro Ghosh. Public records also associate the same name and ClinZen role with CIOReview and an ACN Newswire interview. These are potentially valuable professional corroboration assets because the name, company, and role align with the current resume.

They are **mentions, not verified backlinks to the current domain**. They should not be converted into structured `sameAs`, copied as testimonials, or represented as endorsements. The owner should confirm identity and permissions, then link to factual records from the resume/project context where appropriate.
