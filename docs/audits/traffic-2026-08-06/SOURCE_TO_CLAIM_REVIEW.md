# Source-to-claim editorial triage

Audit date: **2026-08-06 (Asia/Calcutta)**

This is a local editorial reading of the **14 canonical articles that contain authored external Markdown links**. It asks whether each cited target is positioned beside a plausibly matching claim and whether consequential sections have mapped support. It does not establish that a source is live, accurate, current, primary, or sufficient; bounded HTTP observations are reported separately in `CITATION_LIVENESS.csv` and `CITATION_REVIEW.md`.

The other canonical articles were not assigned a semantic correspondence status merely because they contain no authored external link. Factual, technical, scientific, medical, public-health, political, financial, and current-event claims still require page-level editorial review. No reference was added, removed, or edited during this audit.

## Status definitions

- **ALIGNED — LOCAL CONTEXT:** authored sources sit beside narrow, topically matching claims and the article states relevant limits. External truth and sufficiency remain unverified.
- **PARTIAL — COVERAGE OR MAPPING GAP:** useful sources are present, but consequential sections or named claims lack a clear claim-level source.
- **CONCERN — MATERIAL SUPPORT GAP:** the local citation pattern is too generic, ambiguous, or weak for consequential claims and needs editorial correction before promotion.
