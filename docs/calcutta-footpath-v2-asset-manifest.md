# Calcutta Footpath Simulator V2 — asset and licence manifest

The V2 runtime does not download third-party models, textures, photographs, music or field recordings. Its world assets are local: authored Three.js geometry, original procedural canvas textures, one original generated and inspected plaster albedo, and procedural Web Audio. This keeps the game self-contained, avoids unclear street-photography licensing and prevents copyrighted music or geography-mismatched stock sound from entering the mix.

Runtime-generated assets must be inspected in browser captures for tiling, malformed text, impossible geometry, unwanted marks or trademark-like content before release.

## Generated material record

The façade source was generated in the built-in image tool from this material brief: “Seamless, neutral old North Kolkata lime-plaster wall material with restrained weathering and small areas of exposed handmade brick; flat albedo lighting; no text, people, symbols, brands, doors, windows, objects or perspective.” The source was visually inspected before being resized, compressed and passed through the repository image optimizer. The generated image supplies surface variation only; all openings, signs, architectural details, roughness and scene lighting remain authored by the runtime.
