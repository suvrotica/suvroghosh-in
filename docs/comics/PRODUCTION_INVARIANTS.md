## Premise and ending

Golmohar welcomes the Global Municipal Optimization Mission because it promises money, prestige,
jobs, drainage, transport, and better healthcare. Riju Dhar hopes his record-reconciliation
prototype will earn him a permanent municipal post. The system genuinely improves medicine
tracking, train information, clinic results, welfare access, and spending transparency.

Those gains bind previously separate systems together. Clean categories erase landmark addresses,
misread seasonal migration, reward healthcare compliance over urgency, route traffic through a
monsoon-disappearing lane, and block the manual corrections needed to fix the errors. The crisis
peaks when electricity is cut to the municipal server room because the municipal building is
absent from the utility database.

The solution is not sabotage. Golmohar and Vale negotiate an enforceable hybrid trial: systems
must show uncertainty and failure conditions; `unknown` must differ from `false`; consequential
scores require appeal and human recourse; local-language explanations and offline fallbacks are
mandatory; identity and address errors are correctable; overrides are reasoned and auditable; no
single score controls a life; and designers must observe real work before digitising it.

Vale’s report concludes:

> Golmohar Junction remains noncompliant with several fundamental assumptions.

Ila frames it. Cecil reports that the frame is 1.7 degrees off-level. Ila answers:

> That is how we know the wall is still standing.

The exact final exchange may be shortened only if page rhythm requires it; the visual joke and
meaning must remain.

## Story quality

- Page 1 opens on a concrete contradiction, not exposition: a “GOING FORWARD TOGETHER” smart-town
  billboard blocks one traffic lane.
- Riju has a personal stake independent of the civic mission and makes consequential mistakes and
  repairs.
- Every page changes the situation through action, discovery, reversal, choice, or consequence.
- Local procedures remain intelligible from action and context.
- The system becomes frightening by continuing to operate as designed, not by becoming evil.
- Serious harms receive emotional weight. Jokes target institutional vanity, power, opportunism,
  and rigid logic—not poverty, disability, accents, skin tone, informal labour, or victims.
- The town’s workarounds include both humane adaptations and corrupt or exclusionary practices.
- Vale is sincere, capable, and occasionally correct. Cecil remains a non-human categorical
  intelligence, not a human personality in a robot shell.
- Background jokes reward rereading, but essential story information never depends on generated
  signage or untranslated language.

## Cultural and language boundaries

- Golmohar is not Howrah’s Golmohar Railway Quarters or any disguised real locality. Maps remain
  contradictory and no real district is assigned.
- `Calcutta` is the site’s chosen English thematic form; `Kolkata` may occur where an official
  modern name or quotation requires it.
- Bengali words are selective, contextual, and not automatically italicised.
- Final Bengali signage uses real Unicode Bengali text added during deterministic composition;
  image models receive blank sign areas.
- Every publication-bound translation requires a named human reviewer and date in the signage
  manifest. Until then its state is `needs-human-review`.
- Religious, caste, party, and regional symbols are never decorative shorthand.

## Production and accessibility

- Source YAML/JSON is canonical; compiled web data, prompts, transcripts, page SVGs, contact sheets,
  PDF, and EPUB are deterministic derivatives.
- Each panel carries composition, visible cast, location, action, emotion, props, dialogue,
  balloon order, visual joke, continuity, prompt inputs, alt text, and expanded description.
- Lettering is deterministic HTML/SVG. Generated art contains no words, balloons, signatures,
  logos, or watermarks.
- Mobile defaults to guided panel mode. Desktop supports page and optional spread views.
- Dialogue exists once in the accessibility tree. Transcript order must equal page/panel order.
- Missing art renders a contextual production card containing page/panel ID, camera, cast, action,
  and status—not a blank rectangle.
- Raw, rejected, and print-master art stays outside `static`. Public assets are optimized,
  content-versioned derivatives with provenance.

## Completion evidence

Completion requires the repository’s existing checks plus comic validation, prompt coverage,
continuity and cultural reports, transcript parity, accessible reader interaction, print render
inspection, search/feed/sitemap integration, plural-route redirects, no superseded canon strings,
and an honest record of any remaining human artwork or editorial gate.
