## Page 01 — Going forward, one lane at a time

- **Purpose:** Open on the album's central contradiction and give Riju an immediate practical problem.
- **Suggested panels:** 5
- **Location / time:** Junction Square (`junction-square`), late morning.
- **Action:** A new smart-town billboard reserves a clean rectangle for **GOING FORWARD TOGETHER**, but its support blocks one of the square's two usable traffic lanes. Riju, wearing a temporary municipal badge that expires in four days, tries to make the installation crew shift it without delaying the Mission ceremony. Babul-da's loaded cycle van becomes the first vehicle trapped by the promise of progress.
- **Dialogue goal:** Establish Riju's anxious investment in a flawless launch and Babul-da's calm awareness that the town will route around the mistake.
- **Visual joke:** The painted forward arrow points directly into the billboard support while traffic improvises a backward loop.
- **Continuity:** Present IDs: `riju-dhar`, `babul-courier`. Location ID: `junction-square`. Keep the billboard's lettering area blank in art; add all words during composition. Riju's expiring badge and Babul-da's modified cycle van recur.
- **Page-turn function:** A geometric convoy appears at the far end of the blocked lane.
