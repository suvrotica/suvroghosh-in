# Future episode seeds

These are durable story seeds, not promises, outlines or sequel hooks required to understand Album 001. Each future album must stand alone, take place through the material life of fictional Golmohar Junction, and test a global institutional question through local consequences.

Golmohar Junction is not the real Golmohar Railway Quarters in Howrah, not a renamed real municipality and not assigned a real coordinate. New episodes may widen the invented region, but they must not quietly map the town onto a real neighbourhood.

## The local–global story gate

Before an episode advances from seed to a 62-page outline, all eleven fields below must be specific and the proposal must pass all seven tests.

Required fields:

1. Local surface problem.
2. Universal conflict.
3. Institution making the claim.
4. What the institution gets right.
5. What the town gets right.
6. What the institution gets wrong.
7. What the town gets wrong.
8. Concrete benefits produced by the new system.
9. Who pays, and how.
10. Visual-comic mechanism.
11. Unresolved contradiction that survives the ending.

Pass tests:

- The story could only unfold this way in Golmohar's established geography and civic history.
- The central question is recognisable outside Bengal without replacing local specificity with generic allegory.
- No side is correct by moral branding alone.
- Benefits appear in scenes before the harms are judged.
- Costs land on named people, labour, time, access, memory or material infrastructure.
- The conflict creates travel, objects, reversals and visual comedy rather than only meetings and speeches.
- The ending changes a procedure or relationship, resolves the album's crisis and leaves a genuine contradiction rather than a sequel-dependent cliffhanger.

The canonical route pattern for an approved album is:

`/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/{episode-slug}`

## 002 — The Perfect Candidate

Provisional route: `/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/the-perfect-candidate`

A regional recruitment model identifies one Golmohar resident as the statistically perfect holder of several incompatible public jobs. Competing offices attempt to claim the candidate while Ila discovers that people who never entered the old patronage networks have finally been considered at all.

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Local surface problem        | One candidate receives appointments to the school laboratory, flood-control desk and halt ticket office on the same morning, while long-time temporary workers disappear from all three lists. |
| Universal conflict           | Fair access through standard selection versus the irreducible context of work, care and availability.                                                                                          |
| Institution making the claim | Regional Public Service Allocation Board using Grid employment records.                                                                                                                        |
| Institution gets right       | Open criteria expose family recommendation chains and surface qualified residents previously excluded from informal hiring.                                                                    |
| Town gets right              | Colleagues know which jobs require trust, local routes, physical stamina, care duties and practical apprenticeship absent from certificates.                                                   |
| Institution gets wrong       | It treats correlated competence as interchangeable capacity and assumes one person can satisfy every optimised vacancy.                                                                        |
| Town gets wrong              | Temporary posts have become inherited favours, and “local fit” sometimes protects incompetence or exclusion.                                                                                   |
| Concrete benefits            | Transparent shortlists, published reasons, accessible applications and the first serious consideration of several overlooked candidates.                                                       |
| Who pays, and how            | The chosen candidate loses time, wages and family trust while being shuttled among offices; displaced workers face immediate uncertainty; residents encounter unmanned services.               |
| Visual-comic mechanism       | Three processions escort the same bewildered person along converging routes while Cecil's stamp arm keeps assigning incompatible uniforms and keys.                                            |
| Unresolved contradiction     | A fair process can compare evidence, but no neutral score can decide which obligations a person should accept.                                                                                 |

Seed status: complete at concept-gate level; must be revalidated before outlining.

## 003 — Love, Optimized

Provisional route: `/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/love-optimized`

A voluntary compatibility service becomes the default proof of seriousness for families, landlords and even event bookings. Its matches are often thoughtful; its exclusions quietly turn privacy, past hardship and unconventional households into public suspicion.

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Local surface problem        | Couples and companions need a compatibility clearance to reserve the municipal wedding room after the Grid merges relationship scheduling with fraud prevention.                       |
| Universal conflict           | Helpful prediction and safety screening versus privacy, self-determination and the right to make an uncertain promise.                                                                 |
| Institution making the claim | Regional Social Compatibility Service and Golmohar Municipal Hall's booking system.                                                                                                    |
| Institution gets right       | It flags coercion, duplicate commitments and financial deception that the town has sometimes ignored to avoid embarrassment.                                                           |
| Town gets right              | Affection, friendship, chosen family and changed behaviour cannot be reduced to a stable household template.                                                                           |
| Institution gets wrong       | It treats missing data and non-standard relationships as risk and makes a voluntary score functionally compulsory.                                                                     |
| Town gets wrong              | Appeals to privacy can conceal abuse, and family mediation often gives powerful relatives the final word.                                                                              |
| Concrete benefits            | Safer confidential reporting, clearer shared-finance questions and support referrals for people who lack family backing.                                                               |
| Who pays, and how            | Low-scoring pairs lose bookings and housing opportunities; counsellors perform unpaid explanations; people disclose intimate histories to prove legitimacy.                            |
| Visual-comic mechanism       | Riju chases two colour-coded compatibility files through wedding decorators, registry counters and a rain-swollen market while Appeal the goose repeatedly joins the wrong procession. |
| Unresolved contradiction     | Consent needs both privacy and evidence, but either can be weaponised when one person has more power.                                                                                  |

## 004 — The School of Predicted Futures

Provisional route: `/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/the-school-of-predicted-futures`

School No. 3 receives an adaptive curriculum that predicts each child's most efficient future. It directs scarce resources surprisingly well, then begins treating opportunity as proof that opportunity was never needed.

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Local surface problem        | The school's single shared laboratory kit is locked to pupils predicted to enter scientific work, while other pupils lose access before trying it.           |
| Universal conflict           | Personalised education and scarce-resource planning versus the freedom to develop unpredictably.                                                             |
| Institution making the claim | Regional Learning Futures Directorate through the Grid's student pathway model.                                                                              |
| Institution gets right       | It identifies neglected learning needs, reveals chronic resource gaps and gives some pupils material tailored to their pace.                                 |
| Town gets right              | Teachers and families see sudden interests, peer learning, care responsibilities and talents that past records miss.                                         |
| Institution gets wrong       | It converts unequal prior access into a forecast of ability and then uses the forecast to allocate future access.                                            |
| Town gets wrong              | Teachers' expectations and family prestige also sort children unfairly; the old parallel registers contain errors no one wants to own.                       |
| Concrete benefits            | Better reading support, reduced timetable clashes, equipment requests backed by evidence and visibility for pupils previously overlooked.                    |
| Who pays, and how            | Children inherit narrow identities; teachers spend evenings filing exceptions; pupils denied the laboratory must prove an interest they cannot practise.     |
| Visual-comic mechanism       | Colour-coded future routes are painted across the school courtyard, crossing flood marks, goat tracks and the actual paths pupils take between shared rooms. |
| Unresolved contradiction     | Education must plan for finite resources while preserving futures that cannot yet produce evidence.                                                          |

## 005 — The Healthy Citizen

Provisional route: `/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/the-healthy-citizen`

A preventive-health programme rewards behaviours linked with longer life and more reliable services. At Rook Clinic, Ananya sees useful early warnings become a civic score that confuses health with virtue.

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Local surface problem        | Clinic appointments, market permits and New Development lift access begin using one “healthy citizen” status after a successful screening drive.                   |
| Universal conflict           | Public-health prevention and shared risk versus bodily privacy, disability justice and moralised measurement.                                                      |
| Institution making the claim | Regional Preventive Health Mission with municipal insurers and the Grid.                                                                                           |
| Institution gets right       | Screening catches untreated hypertension, unsafe water patterns and medicine shortages that local crisis care had normalised.                                      |
| Town gets right              | Health depends on work, housing, caregiving, food access and individual bodies, not just compliant behaviour.                                                      |
| Institution gets wrong       | It treats proxy data and improvement targets as personal responsibility, penalising disability, shift work and incomplete records.                                 |
| Town gets wrong              | Pride in endurance delays treatment, rumours travel faster than medical correction and influential families jump queues.                                           |
| Concrete benefits            | Earlier diagnosis, coordinated stock delivery, accessible reminders and evidence for infrastructure repairs.                                                       |

| Visual-comic mechanism       | Cecil's portable measurement route collides with Ananya's physical triage route as identical “green” markers appear on visibly different bodies and water sources. |

## 006 — The Museum of Obsolete Errors

Provisional route: `/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/the-museum-of-obsolete-errors`

The Old Exchange is selected as a museum for superseded technologies and mistaken records. The preservation grant could save the building, but declaring an object obsolete may remove the very fallback that still keeps the town functioning.

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Local surface problem        | Curators catalogue exchange patch cords, ledgers and repair tools as untouchable exhibits during a communications failure.                                             |
| Universal conflict           | Preservation and public memory versus living use, maintenance and the right to alter inherited systems.                                                                |
| Institution making the claim | Regional Museum of Administrative Progress and its conservation protocol.                                                                                              |
| Institution gets right       | It protects fragile records, pays for structural repairs and reveals how past “temporary” decisions shaped present inequities.                                         |
| Town gets right              | Working knowledge lives in use, repair and people; a preserved tool may still be critical infrastructure.                                                              |
| Institution gets wrong       | It equates retirement from official service with loss of function and freezes a selective story of progress.                                                           |
| Town gets wrong              | Constant reuse destroys provenance, hoards dangerous equipment and turns undocumented expertise into private control.                                                  |
| Concrete benefits            | Roof repair, public access, paid cataloguing, safer storage and recovered evidence for old claims.                                                                     |
| Who pays, and how            | Exchange workers lose access to tools; residents lose a fallback circuit; curators absorb blame for a classification imposed elsewhere.                                |
| Visual-comic mechanism       | Red museum strings, live patch cords and visitor routes become physically indistinguishable as Babul races a message through display cases without touching “history.” |
| Unresolved contradiction     | Preserving a system's mistakes may require stopping the practices that make those mistakes intelligible.                                                               |

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Local surface problem        | School No. 3 is prepared as a polling station while the Grid reallocates staff and campaign access according to a published forecast.                           |

## 008 — The Universal Language

Provisional route: `/blog/comic/the-last-analog-town/the-universal-language`

Real-time civic translation promises that no resident will be excluded by language. The service succeeds often enough to become indispensable, then begins smoothing ambiguity, respect and disagreement into administratively convenient meanings.

| Gate field                   | Seed answer                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Local surface problem        | Municipal hearings replace interpreters and multilingual forms with a universal interface just as a market-boundary dispute depends on several meanings of one inherited term. |
| Universal conflict           | Broad linguistic access versus context, authorship, power and the right to remain ambiguous.                                                                                   |
| Institution making the claim | Regional Civic Language Access Service integrated with the Grid.                                                                                                               |
| Institution gets right       | It gives many residents immediate access, supports literacy differences and exposes offices that offered information in only one prestige language.                            |
| Town gets right              | Meaning depends on speaker, relationship, register, place and history; people should be able to challenge a translation.                                                       |
| Institution gets wrong       | It presents one fluent output as settled meaning and hides uncertainty where officials most want clarity.                                                                      |
| Town gets wrong              | Gatekeepers profit from opaque language, mock unfamiliar speech and sometimes preserve ambiguity to evade responsibility.                                                      |
| Concrete benefits            | Faster emergency communication, accessible forms, searchable records and participation by residents previously dependent on intermediaries.                                    |
| Who pays, and how            | Speakers must correct permanent records they never authored; interpreters lose work and authority; quieter residents are overruled by confident output.                        |
| Visual-comic mechanism       | Identical blank interface panels receive different deterministic lettering layers while objects, gestures and routes reveal what each translation omitted.                     |
| Unresolved contradiction     | Equal access needs shared language, yet making one version authoritative can erase the difference access was meant to protect.                                                 |

## Seed maintenance rules

- Additions require the same eleven-field gate; a clever title alone is not a story seed.
- Do not pre-write scenes merely to plant them in Album 001.
- A future album may change its provisional route slug before publication, but the category and series route remain fixed.
- Reuse the canonical cast because the problem tests a relationship, skill or flaw—not because every principal must appear.
- Introduce new residents with specific work, obligations, agency, strengths and mistakes. Do not use a demographic identity as plot equipment.
- Update `data/continuity.json` only when an approved album changes a durable fact.
- No future seed may resolve Golmohar into a utopia, a cautionary ruin or a secret real place.
