Want something on while you make tea, work or sit by the window? Press **Play**, or choose a station from the list.

## Start listening

Start with **FM Rainbow Kolkata**. Select it from the list, give the stream a moment to connect, and set the volume to a comfortable level. The display tells you when it is playing. **Stop** ends playback; choosing another station replaces the current one.

For Bengali programmes, try **Geetanjali**, **Sanchayita**, **Akashvani Bangla** or **Maitree**. The list also includes Kolkata and national Vividh Bharati services.

Type **English** in the search box for English-language options. **WNYC** carries news and conversation; **KEXP** offers independent music; **WQXR** plays classical music. Their locations are shown beside their names.

## Keep your favourites close

Press the star beside a station to save it, then choose **Favourites** to see your saved list. Your choices and sound settings stay in this browser when storage is available. There is no account to create.

The sleep timer stops the radio after **15**, **30** or **60 minutes**. Changing stations keeps the same deadline. Choose **Off** to cancel the timer. On an iPhone or iPad, use the device's volume buttons.

## When a station is unavailable

A familiar station name does not guarantee an internet stream. **More stations on their official sites** has links to Mirchi, RED FM and BIG FM, where you can check their own listening options.

If a stream fails, use **Retry station**, choose another station or open its official listening site. Streams can change, and availability depends on the broadcaster, your connection and your browser.

## About this radio

The dark panels and amber display take their cue from a physical radio. The two dials show volume and the sleep timer; the clock shows Calcutta time.

This is internet radio, so it needs a connection. Playing a station connects your browser to its stream provider, which supplies the programme and any broadcast advertising. Calcutta Radio does not record or rebroadcast the audio.
