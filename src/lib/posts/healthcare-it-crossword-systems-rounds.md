## A crossword with a patient tutor inside it

Systems Rounds is a compact learning crossword for resurfacing healthcare IT knowledge. Choose a topic, a difficulty, and a session length; then work through clues about interoperability, clinical systems, terminology, research data, data engineering, analytics, governance, and responsible AI. Forgetting is expected. It is, inconveniently, one of the reasons the game exists.

## How a round works

Use a physical keyboard, touch the grid, or work from the accessible clue list. Coach mode quietly identifies letters that do not fit. Traditional mode waits until you ask to check a letter, an answer, or the puzzle. Pencil marks keep uncertain answers visibly tentative.

Hints become progressively more explicit: first a conceptual nudge, then a plain-language bridge, a useful contrast, strategic letter help, and finally a reveal with a short teaching card. A reveal is not treated as a theatrical failure. The concept simply joins the local review queue and returns in a later round.

## Progress and privacy

Unfinished rounds, settings, hint use, and review history remain in this browser. The game requires no account, runtime clue generation, patient data, or remote learner profile. Saved learning data can be exported, imported, or cleared from the game settings.
