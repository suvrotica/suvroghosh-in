<p>
I wanted a life<br />
it didn't work<br />
I wanted to write<br />
the bots had pens<br />
I wanted to work<br />
no one hires honest men<br />
So I stay home<br />
and lie flat all day.
</p>

<p>
The world is changed<br />
I didn't change it.<br />
Change happens slowly<br />
I thought<br />
I was wrong.<br />
Everything went from this to that<br />
in small change of years,<br />
I stood where I was<br />
half dead<br />
buried in yesterday's thoughts.
</p>

<p>
Now I mutter to the wind<br />
and they say I'm mad.<br />
I may be<br />
and that's quite all right now.<br />
These are the times<br />
when madness seems really right.<br />
And I am one<br />
already halfway through the stairs.
</p>

<p>
I never knew<br />
what I was saying<br />
ever,<br />
Even now,<br />
I don't really know<br />
where I'm going with this.<br />
But if I'm going mad<br />
or if crazy is the way the wind blows,<br />
Then hey,<br />
the words sing just the right tune<br />
I think.
</p>
