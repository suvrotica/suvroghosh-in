## Two faces of one inexpensive board

This page reconstructs the sort of folding cardboard board that could spend years in a Calcutta cupboard: Ludo printed on one face, Saap-Ludo on the other, a little rubbed at the hinge, and ready to occupy an afternoon. It is an original representative reconstruction, not a copy of a surviving commercial board and not a claim that every Calcutta family used one rulebook.

## Ludo

Four guti begin in each yard. A six brings one out or moves an active guti, and the same unbiased die serves the human and every computer player. Starts and printed rosettes are safe; ordinary exact landings capture; two matching guti can form a blockade; and an exact throw is needed to reach the centre. A third consecutive six is discarded after the first two moves remain played.

The computer opponents know only the public board. They value home, captures, safe squares, useful spread and blockades, add a little seeded uncertainty, and never inspect a future throw. The human must always roll and choose a legal token personally.

## Saap-Ludo

Every counter begins off the board and normally needs exactly one to enter square 1. The numbered route alternates direction across ten rows. Exact landings alone use the eight fixed ladders or eight fixed snakes; passing a foot or head does nothing. A six grants another throw after entry, several counters may share a square, and square 100 requires an exact roll.

## Saved locally, played patiently

The two faces keep independent versioned matches in this browser. Switching faces or hiding the page pauses computer presentation delays; returning resumes one intelligible next action rather than releasing a queue. The committed die, seeded random state, tokens, counters, opening order, house rules and short move log survive a reload. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account, remote opponent, leaderboard, advertising, game currency or game-specific telemetry.

Sound is off by default. The boards, controls, full-size legal-move buttons, concise narration and move logs remain usable by keyboard, touch and screen reader. Reduced-motion settings replace counted travel with brief settling while preserving every logical result.
