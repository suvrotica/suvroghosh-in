There is no music. I wake up and the silence is not golden, it is grey, the grey of wet newspaper, the grey of a pigeon that has given up on flight and decided to waddle through its remaining days with the dignity of a damp sock. I lie there, a decaying moribund morbid manifestation haunting my own skull, and I wait for the cinematic score to kick in, the violins, the timpani, the swelling brass that tells the audience that the protagonist matters. Nothing. Just the hum of the refrigerator, that loyal idiot, keeping my leftovers cold while I cannot keep anything warm.

I eat. I excrete. I fall asleep to nightmares and wake early to depressive doom. The blog sits there, a digital monument to my own obscurity, read by no one, or perhaps by three people who stumbled upon it while searching for something else entirely, pornography or tax forms or the correct spelling of "diarrhea." I am barely passing as someone alive, a forgery of a man, a photocopy of a photocopy of a person, the contrast fading with each replication until there is only a faint outline where a face should be.

They tell us, the fat textbooks do, that we are matter, and matter is conserved. $E = mc^2$, that beautiful bastard of an equation, promising that energy becomes mass and mass becomes energy, that nothing is lost, only transformed. But I look at my own transformation, this slow slide from potential to residue, and I wonder where the energy went. The conservation of misery, perhaps. The entropy of a closed system tends to increase. My room is a closed system. I am the entropy.

I have no family living with me here, no friends ringing the bell, no one who needs me to fix their leaking tap or listen to their marital disasters. I am not a cog in any machine. I am the grease that flaked off the cog years ago, lying in the corner of the factory floor, wondering if I was ever part of anything that turned. The greater society outside my shanty bedroom walls does not know I exist, and if it did, it would not care. I am a rounding error in the census, a statistical ghost, a zero that dreams of being a negative number just to have some magnitude, some direction, some proof that it is not merely nothing but actively less than nothing.

And yet.

The alarm rings. I rise. I perform the drudgery, the chore of keeping this meat operational, this bag of mostly water and regret that insists on breathing. Why? Not for joy. Not for purpose. Perhaps for the same reason Sisyphus rolled his boulder, not because he hoped to reach the top, but because the rolling itself was the last defiant gesture against the silence. Camus called it absurd. I call it Tuesday. Or Wednesday. The days are interchangeable, a deck of cards all marked with the same blank face.

I write this blog that no one reads, and in the writing, there is a small, mean satisfaction. Not happiness, no, that is a country from which I have been deported, but a kind of spiteful productivity. Take that, universe. I have strung words together. I have made a sentence that did not exist before. It is not a child, it is not a cure for cancer, it is not even a good sentence, perhaps, but it is mine, this small turd of consciousness dropped on the doorstep of oblivion.

The night comes. The nightmares queue up like impatient customers at a government office, each one clutching a number, waiting to be called to the window of my sleeping mind. I wake before dawn, the depressive doom sitting on my chest like a cat that has mistaken me for furniture. There is no score. There never was. But I am still here, unfortunately, inexplicably, stubbornly here, a ghost that refuses to vacate the premises.
