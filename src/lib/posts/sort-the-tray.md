## A few things to put in their place

A coin goes into the Coins dish. A button goes into Buttons. A stone goes into Stones. Six objects are ready when you open the page.

There is no timer, score or increasing difficulty. A misplaced object stays available, and the finished tray waits until you choose another. Play for a moment or stay a while.

## How to sort

Drag an object to its labelled dish. Or tap the object, then the dish. With a keyboard, Tab to an object and press Enter or Space. Focus moves to the dishes; choose one the same way. Escape clears a selection.

Another tray appears when everything is sorted. Your progress stays saved if you leave.

## Make yourself comfortable

Sound starts off. Turn it on for quiet placement sounds, or keep your own music or spoken audio playing. Objects have distinct shapes as well as colours. Options includes reduced motion, and the game also respects your device setting.

## Saved in this browser

Your tray and preferences stay in this browser. No account or download is needed, and game progress is not uploaded. A different browser or device starts its own tray. If storage is unavailable, you can still play.
