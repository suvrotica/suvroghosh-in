## Make something you did not expect

Start with **Liquid Knot**: a folded, iridescent form that catches the light as it turns. Move **Shape** a little. Change the colour. Pause when the silhouette feels right, then drag the artwork to find another side.

The other worlds begin with different structures. **Alien Bloom** grows a layered organic form. **Neon Current** gathers luminous threads into a moving field. **Impossible Tunnel** repeats and bends geometry into a passage with no obvious end.

**Surprise me** chooses another constrained variation. If you liked the previous one better, use **Undo**. There is no correct arrangement to discover; a small change can make the same starting rules feel entirely different.

## Six controls, plenty of possibilities

**Shape** changes the structure. **Energy** changes the strength of its movement. **Detail** changes its complexity, **Glow** its luminosity, and **Speed** how quickly time passes. **Colour** brings a different palette to the same form.

You can pause the motion and keep exploring with the camera. People who request reduced motion begin with a still composition and can choose to play it.

## Keep a discovery

Choose a landscape, square or portrait composition before saving a PNG. The export contains the artwork without the controls. Compatible browsers can also record a short animation; the recording option shows its actual file format.

Copy the artwork link to keep its seed, settings, camera and moment in the animation. The same procedural design can vary slightly in its final pixels across graphics hardware and browsers.

These are digital sculptures, made from mathematical forms and authored visual choices. They are an invitation to experiment with colour, light and movement.
