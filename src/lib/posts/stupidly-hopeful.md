<Pi src="/images/art-stupidly-hopeful.jpg" alt="A framed black-and-white caricature of a weary man with round glasses and a cigarette, hanging on a pale wall." />

I think about my hopes.<br />
Expectations are low.<br />
Lower than my systolic,<br />
Life's tough on me, bro!

I say that as I smirk.<br />
Bad poetry pairs with luck.<br />
It's been very long since<br />
I've felt somewhat unstuck.

Just hours roll into days,<br />
Then those into weeks to years.<br />
Fifty-one, yes, I am over the hill.<br />
Scars I carry; no one really cares.

But I can't sleep. I wake up<br />
Every few minutes with a worry.<br />
Depression and anxiety, my pals,<br />
Neither good-looking nor furry.

But I still hope; that's my constant.<br />
I am a stupidly hopeful guy.<br />
That before I die I will have<br />
Some reason to happily die.
