## About the game

Run a small repair shop on fictional Kusumtala Corner in a present-day Calcutta lane where everyday objects stay in working life. Neighbours describe symptoms rather than announcing the answer: Bapi from the corner tea stall brings a portable battery radio whose power lamp stays lit while the audio disappears whenever the case moves; Mala Sen next door has a wind-up clock that takes spring tension cleanly while its hands stand still; and Ruma Chatterjee needs an 8mm projector mended before the courtyard film evening.

Every job follows the same unhurried sequence: listen to the symptom, test and observe what still works, switch off and open the casing at the wooden workbench, identify the genuine fault, repair it reversibly, verify it under test, and return it to see the neighbourhood change around you.

## How to play and controls

Walk through the repair shop, lane, Sen Brothers counter, tea stall, and shared courtyard using **WASD** or the **Arrow keys**, by clicking or tapping any reachable spot on the ground, or via the labelled **Walk & Visit** buttons. Press **E** or **Enter** when nearby (or tap the highlighted prompt) to talk with neighbours, inspect your spare-parts drawers, or step up to the workbench.

At the workbench, drag or use the camera controls to rotate and zoom around the three-dimensional object. Every screw, cover, connector, brass wheel, belt, film guide, and dial can be operated either by clicking or dragging on the 3D model or through the semantic HTML controls beside the view. **Escape** steps back from the workbench, closes a conversation, or opens **Workshop Options**. Three optional hint levels and a dedicated **Reset this repair** control carry no penalty.

## Accessibility and browser-local saves

Sound begins off unless you choose to enter with workshop audio, and every audible clue—such as radio dropouts, tuning lock, clockwork ticks, and the evening short film—is paired with clear visual indicators and live text captions. High-contrast labels, keyboard-only play, touch controls without hover, and reduced-motion preferences are supported across desktop and mobile screens.

Your accepted jobs, workbench component states, returned objects, neighbourhood unlocks, and comfort settings stay privately in this browser's `localStorage`. You can pause or close the tab after a single repair and continue later without an account, cloud upload, or external tracking.
