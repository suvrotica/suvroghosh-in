The blank page is not white. It is the color of accusation.

It sits on the screen in my room at the edge of Calcutta, bright, clean, and faintly insulting. Outside, the city continues producing material without mercy: vendors, heat, drains, political noise, unpaid labor, broken promises, price changes, old men with opinions, young people with phones, and the general civic music of things not being fixed. Yet I sit there with nothing.

Nothing is not emptiness. Nothing has texture.

It is the hour after tea when the cup has cooled and the mind refuses to become useful. It is the day when every subject feels either too large or too stale. Politics? Too loud. Technology? Too fast. Personal history? Too sticky. Science? Too dignified for the condition of the table. The page waits while the writer becomes a clerk in the office of his own delay.

Writing about nothing sounds like fraud, but it may be the most honest subject on certain days. Not every day contains a theme. Some days contain only a room, a fan, a bill, a headache, a half-washed cup, and the low-grade astonishment that life expects continuity without providing a proper script.

The internet dislikes nothing. It prefers hooks, takes, angles, lessons, conclusions, urgency. A post should solve, provoke, teach, announce, rank, expose, or confess. Nothing refuses all that. It says: here is a man in a room, not ready, not heroic, not decorative, still trying to assemble one paragraph from the lint of the day.

There is a kind of dignity in that, though a very small one.

A sentence can be a handrail. You do not build a palace with it. You use it to cross a damp patch without falling. One sentence, then another. The subject arrives late, if it arrives at all. Sometimes the subject is simply the act of remaining at the desk.

Calcutta understands such partial repairs. A wire is tied. A leak is slowed. A door is wedged. A bus still runs though no philosopher would approve of its metaphysics. The city survives by imperfect continuations.

So does writing.

The page is still mostly blank. But now it has a few footprints across it.
